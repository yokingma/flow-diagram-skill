# Emphasis colors and flow highlighting

[Diagram JSON](emphasis-flow.json) · [Example index](README.md)

Before running or exporting, read the [shared execution and viewing conditions](../README.md#run-and-inspect).

**Reader's question:** How do palette and automatic flow highlights interact?

**Source scenario:** Compare three sender-to-receiver transfers: automatic highlights, preserved relationship colors, and marker-only motion. A document palette supplies mint fills, teal highlights and white marker text across themes and exports.

**Composition and animation:** Three sender/receiver rows compare automatic highlights, preserved ordinary edge color and marker-only motion. Reduced motion can leave marker-only transfers without an automatic visible cue.

**Static reading:** Use scene `compare` for simultaneous flows. The opening is the static palette overview.

**Scenes:** `palette`, `automatic`, `preserve-edge`, `marker-only`, `compare`, `complete`.

**Canvas:** 920 × 420 diagram units.
