# Content cards

[Diagram source](content-cards.json)

**Question:** How can comparable objects combine paragraphs, lists and short tags while retaining a clear reading order?

**Teaching facts:** The teaching topics are source organization, visual organization and review at the target size. They distinguish facts from unknowns, measurement from authored height choices, and tool checks from actual viewing. They describe diagram-authoring methods rather than external business facts.

**Why this composition:** Three columns share internal left alignment and padding, with a title, paragraph and list reading order. Equal-height cards reserve room for the English paragraphs and lists while keeping the type size. Groups provide card boundaries, and list blocks preserve list semantics. Each decorative tag combines a rounded background with a separate, visually centered text node; these nodes add layout detail rather than business objects. No edges are needed because comparison does not establish execution dependencies.

**Display and limits:** Static light-background diagram, 1100 × 432 units, intended for a 1100 × 432 PNG with actual HTML displayed at a 1440 × 1000 browser viewport. Use it for comparable content with mixed text structures. Keep facts and readable type when adapting content, then measure and view the new result.

For current viewing coverage and unperformed checks, read the [library viewing scope](README.md#display-conditions-and-limits).
