# Sources mapped to services

[Diagram source](source-services.json)

**Question:** How can source counts and service responsibilities share a diagram without implying item-by-item calls or delivery commitments?

**Teaching facts:** This fictional example has one category of public guides, two consultation channels (counter and online), and 21 candidate questions awaiting verification. The source collection maps as a whole to guide retrieval, consultation routing, question collection and content maintenance. The counts are not delivery commitments. Individual mappings, runtime dependencies and routing every question to every service are unspecified.

**Why this composition:** The left collection separates count, object and context. Four consistent cards describe the service collection on the right. One horizontal edge meets the boundaries of the two collections; its group-mapping note sits above the path with visible clearance. The service boundary makes the target scope clear. Both boundaries describe information scope rather than deployment or trust boundaries.

**Display and limits:** Static light-background diagram, 1280 × 560 units, intended for a 1200 × 525 PNG with actual HTML displayed at a 1440 × 1000 browser viewport. Use it for an aggregate mapping when individual relationships are unknown. Detailed routing needs additional facts. Adapted content needs fresh measurement and actual viewing.

For current viewing coverage and unperformed checks, read the [library viewing scope](README.md#display-conditions-and-limits).
